# Glossary

- **Asset availability** – Measure of the percentage of scheduled time that equipment is operational and ready to perform its intended function. 

- **Asset management** – Tracking organizational assets like machinery, equipment, and buildings throughout their entire lifecycles. 

- **Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS)** – Large-scale collection of rechargeable batteries that stores surplus electricity from the power grid or renewable sources and discharges it during periods of high demand. 

- **Curtailment** – The deliberate reduction or limitation of output from a power generator, most commonly wind or solar farms, below what it could otherwise produce. 

- **Distributed solar generation** – Producing electricity using small-scale solar panels installed directly at or near the place where the power is consumed. 

- **Energy trading** – The buying and selling of power to balance supply and demand on the power grid. 

- **Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP)** – A software system that businesses use to connect and manage core daily operations like finance, HR, and supply chains in one central place. 

- **Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG)** – A framework used by investors and companies to evaluate an organisation's sustainability, ethical impact, and long-term risk management. 

- **Grid interconnection infrastructure** – The physical and technical network (e.g. high-voltage power lines, substations, transformers, and cross-border cables) that links power generators and consumers to the main electricity grid. 

- **Grid management system** – A software and hardware suite used by electric utilities to monitor, control, and optimize the generation, transmission, and distribution of electricity in real time. 

- **Irradiance** – The measure of the power of electromagnetic radiation (such as light or sunlight) received by a surface per unit area. 

- **Performance Ratio (PR)** – A quality and efficiency metric for solar photovoltaic (PV) power plants that compares actual energy generation to the theoretical maximum output under ideal conditions. 

- **Photovoltaic (PV)** – Converting sunlight directly into electricity using special semiconductor materials. 

- **Power Purchase Agreement (PPA)** – A long-term contract between an electricity generator and a buyer (e.g. utility companies, governments, or corporations) to buy or sell energy at a pre-negotiated price over a period of 5 - 25 years. 

- **Regional operations control center** – A centralized facility that monitors, coordinates, and manages high-voltage electrical transmission systems and power generation in real time. 

- **Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition (SCADA)** – A software and hardware system used by human operators to monitor and control industrial processes using sensors and actuators over a communication network. 

- **Utility-scale solar power** – Large ground-mounted solar farms that generate electricity to sell directly to the wholesale power grid rather than to individual homes or businesses.
